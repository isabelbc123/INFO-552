Why Warm up and Cool Down?

  • Warming up and cooling down can prevent injury and reduce soreness.
  • When warming up, blood flow to and temperature of your muscles increases. This supplies your muscles with oxygen and makes them more flexible.
  • Cooling down allows your heart rate to lower and your blood vessels to constrict at a slower pace. This can prevent against the buildup of lactic acid, which inhibits soreness.
