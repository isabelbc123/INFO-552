Further Learning

Why Cross Train?

  • Climbing uses some muscle groups more than others. This can cause imbalances, which can lead to injury.
  • Strength training can help you build strength faster to push past training plateaus.
  • You can increase your endurance, grip strength, and mobility, all of which will help you climb more effectively.

Cardio

  • Doing cardio can help you build endurance and learn how to control your breathing while under stress
  • Try to do an activity that raises your heart rate 2-3 times a week for 30-60 minutes at a time.

Weight Lifting/ Strength Training

  • Try for a lower weight with more reps because when you are climbing, you are moving continuously. This simulates that pattern.
  • Target your rotator cuff and latissimus dorsi (lats). Your rotator cuff helps stabilize your shoulder, while your lats help you pull your body up. Both of these muscles are very helpful when climbing!
  • Also be sure to add in lower body and core movements, as the body parts help you remain stable while on the wall.
