Welcome!

This website is meant to be a resource for rock climbers looking to learn more about safety and injury prevention when climbing. The information provided is meant to be a broad overview of many different topics as an educational resource. Rock climbing is dangerous, and climbers engage with the sport at their own risk. This information is not intended to be a substitute for professional safety or medical advice. Happy climbing!
