How to Prevent Soreness

  • Drink lots of water. Water helps your body expel waste products, which can be the cause of soreness.
  • Eat a diet high in protein and carbs. Protein helps your muscle fibers rebuild and carbs give you much needed energy.
  • When trying a new exercise, try easing into it. For example, if you are trying a new weight lifting exercise, lift a little lighter than you think you should. This allows your body to adapt to new movements.
  • Warm up and cool down properly. Check out more information about warm up and cool down for climbers.

How to Treat Soreness After It Happens

  • Rest. Make sure you get at least 7-9 hours of sleep every night, and avoid strenuous exercise of the affected area.
  • Get a massage or use a foam roller. A professional massage can help increase blood flow and relieve tension in muscles. If that's not an option, you can give yourself a massage with a foam roller.
  • Continue to exercise lightly. It's important to keep moving in order to increase blood flow to sore muscles, but don't overdo it.
  • Consider using ice packs or heating pads. Coldness can help with pain management and decrease inflammation, while heat helps to increase blood flow.
