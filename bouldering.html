What is Bouldering?

Bouldering is a type of climbing that involves climbing shorter distances without a rope or harness to catch your fall. As a result, it's important to understand how to fall safely and maintain a safe landing area.

Landing Area

  • Before you ascend the wall, make sure your landing area is clear.
  • Don't necessarily place crash pads right up against the wall. Place them where you're most likely to land, which could be further out.
  • Arrange your crash pads to minimize gaps between them.
  • Never walk underneath somebody who is actively bouldering.
  • Whenever possible, climb down the wall instead of jumping or falling.

Proper Falling Technique

  • Try to land on your feet, with your knees bent. You don't want to lock your knees straight because that ensures that your joints will absorb all of the impact.
  • As you are landing, tuck your chin in. Let the momentum carry you onto your back. This way, your entire body absorbs the impact.
  • Always keep your arms close to your chest. This prevents you from bracing the fall with your hands, which can lead to wrist injury.
  • Practice falling before going to the top of a wall. Muscle memory will help you!
